# ClipFarm creative

- `feed-1080x1350.jpg` is the Feed creative.
- `story-1080x1920.jpg` is the vertical Stories creative.
- `key-visual.jpg` is the clean supporting artwork.
- `../../public/assets/clipfarm-hero.webp` is the website hero.
- `build-ads.sh` applies ClipFarm's copy and layout with ImageMagick.

The built-in ImageGen tool generated both pieces of photography. The subjects are fictional. The advertising text was typeset separately.

## ImageGen prompts

### Website hero

Landscape 3:2 brand hero artwork for ClipFarm, an AI tool turning long podcasts into vertical shorts. Premium editorial product photography. A warmly lit modern podcast studio with a single fictional woman host wearing headphones speaking into a black broadcast microphone, captured through a framing motif of one landscape source and two portrait crop frames with sharp lime edges. Charcoal and deep forest background, warm natural skin tones, one electric lime accent. Composition weighted toward right, left quarter dark quiet negative space. Sophisticated design studio art direction, no words, no fake interface, no logos, no metrics, no watermarks.

### Advertising key visual

Portrait 2:3 advertising key visual for ClipFarm, the tool that turns one podcast into short clips. Three staggered vertical crop frames, each showing a different authentic-looking original moment from the same fictional podcast studio (two diverse adult speakers in conversation); dark charcoal environment, fine electric lime edges, intentional editorial lighting and tactile photo realism. Keep upper quarter and lower quarter dark and quiet for real typeset copy added later; artwork center aligned for friendly crop to 4:5 and expansion to 9:16. No letters, no words, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks. Direct, polished DTC social ad imagery.
