Will my video work?

ClipFarm is built for footage where people are talking. Here is the honest list.

Works well

  • Direct HTTPS links to MP4, MOV, or WebM
  • 20 seconds to 60 minutes, up to 800 MB
  • Podcasts, interviews, webinars, and streams with clear speech
  • One speaker or a clean conversation

Won’t work

  • YouTube watch pages
  • Private, expiring, or DRM-protected links
  • Silent gameplay with no commentary
  • Music-only clips with nothing to transcribe

Have a YouTube link? If it’s your own upload, download the file from YouTube Studio and paste a direct link to it. ClipFarm can’t import a watch page.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of videos work best?

Footage where people are talking: podcasts, interviews, webinars, and streams with commentary. Give ClipFarm clean speech and enough context for each moment to stand on its own.

How long is each clip?

Clips are cut around complete thoughts, usually between 20 and 60 seconds. ClipFarm chooses boundaries from the transcript instead of cutting mid-sentence.

How are captions generated? Can I edit them?

Captions are generated from the source audio and burned into the video. They can’t be edited after export.

Does ClipFarm crop my video or add a watermark?

The full frame stays visible. Wide video gets a blurred background above and below to fill the 9:16 frame. No watermark is added.

What happens if it finds fewer clips than I asked for?

ClipFarm returns fewer clips when it can’t find enough usable moments. You only use credits for the clips it finishes.

When do credits get used?

Each finished clip uses 100 credits. Credits for clips that can’t be delivered are returned to your balance.

How long are downloads available?

You can download your clips for seven days after they’re exported.

Can I use footage I did not create?

Only with permission from the rights holder. ClipFarm is a processing tool, not a license to reuse someone else’s video.

What happens after I pay?

Sign in, paste a direct video link, choose how many clips you want, and download the MP4s when the job finishes.
