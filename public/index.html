Frequently asked questions

Can I paste a YouTube watch link?

ClipFarm accepts direct HTTPS links to MP4, MOV, or WebM files, from 20 seconds to 60 minutes. Use a video file you have permission to clip.

When do I use credits?

ClipFarm uses 100 credits per finished clip and returns credits for any clips it can’t deliver.
